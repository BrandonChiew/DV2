Year notes (BOM Annual Climate Statements):
2019 - https://www.bom.gov.au/climate/current/annual/aus/2019/ : "A very strong positive Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD) was one of the main influences on Australia's climate during 2019"; "The El Nino-Southern Oscillation remained neutral throughout 2019"; driest year on record (277.6 mm).
2022 - https://www.bom.gov.au/climate/current/annual/aus/2022/ : La Nina plus a negative Indian Ocean Dipole in winter and spring; ninth-wettest year; wettest spring on record for NSW and Victoria.
